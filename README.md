# Stacker AI Visibility — Concept Dashboard

Each article published to the Stacker newswire impacts brand visibility, but questions remain: What is the real impact?

Until recently, it's been difficult to know which prompts drive citations to specific webpages. By extracting and validating prompts in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster, real prompts can be tagged and tracked for each article published to the Stacker newswire -- like in this demo dashboard: https://colbere.github.io/Stacker-GEO-Dashboard/

## The idea

This repo is a concept mockup for a GEO reporting layer for Stacker, allowing clients to see how their syndicated content is actually performing in AI search. With those prompts used by real searchers, brands can track mentions influenced by each article they publish. This doesn't just track one article to see if it's cited; it tracks the real impact of earned media with prompts that are used by real searchers, a real audience, a real ICP.

## What's in this repo

A single static page (`index.html`) showing what the client-facing "Visibility" view of this reporting layer could look like, including an article selector, a visibility trend chart, and the top prompts driving that visibility, with example AI-response previews on hover.

## ⚠️ All data is fake

Every article, brand, prompt, percentage, and chart value in this demo is made up for illustration only. Nothing here is connected to real Stacker data, real client data, or any live system. This exists purely to show what the product experience could feel like.

## Why this matters for Stacker

Earned media's value has always been visibility and share of voice, not URL-level rank. This reframes GEO reporting around that same idea — and it's a report only Stacker can credibly build, because of the syndication network behind it.
